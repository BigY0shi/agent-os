## Failures and how to do differently

- Symptom: a concept stays too vague to feel worth building -> cause: too many design axes are moving at once -> fix: narrow one axis at a time, especially consequences, team size, death model, loadout model, trap counterplay, and tone. [Task 2]
- Symptom: the design drifts toward heavy classes/operator identity too early -> cause: locking roles before the core loop is proven -> fix: keep freeform loadouts plus soft incentives as the first pass. [Task 2]
